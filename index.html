Question 2:

A compound has the following molecular formula: C5H10O? It has a broad infrared absorption band in the 3200 - 3550 cm-1 region and a sharp absorption band in the 1620 - 1680 cm-1 region. It has two (2) methyl groups and a tertiary functional group.

Which of the following is a possible structure of the compound?

Molecular structure formula: C5H10O, has 2 methyl groups: CH3x2=C2H6. Remaining structure: C5H10O-C2H6 = C3H4O. Broad IR band at 3200 - 3550 cm-1 is contributed by a carbon-carbon double bond(C=C); the remaining structure: C3H4O - C2 = CH4O. Absorption at 1620 - 1680 cm-1 region is contributed by a hydroxyl (OH) group. The OH is attached to a C that is attached to three (3) C atoms. Remaining: CH4O-OH=CH3. The remaining C should be from the tertiary carbon. The remaining three (3) H make up the remaining structure.

Source: Solomons, Organic Chemistry, 7th ed., Section 2.16: Infrared Spectroscopy: An Instrumental Method for Detecting Functional Groups

Question 9: In an acid-base titration, what is the equivalence point?
The equivalence point occurs when the stoichiometric amount of titrant has been added to completely react with the analyte.
Question 10: What does UV-Vis spectroscopy primarily measure?
UV-Vis spectroscopy measures transitions of electrons between energy levels when molecules absorb UV or visible light.
